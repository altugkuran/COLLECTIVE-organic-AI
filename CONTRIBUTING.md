# Contributing to COLLECTIVE

COLLECTIVE is a speculative, interdisciplinary research proposal. Critical reviews and negative findings are welcome.

## Ways to contribute
- Identify a claim that is physically or biologically implausible, with supporting literature.
- Compare a proposed subsystem against demonstrated alternatives.
- Suggest measurable, falsifiable simulation tests and meaningful baselines.
- Analyze reward design, distributed learning, resource constraints, or communication.
- Propose a clearer separation between established results and hypotheses.

## Issue format
**Topic / subsystem:**

**Claim or question:**

**What is known (with citations):**

**Key uncertainty or failure mode:**

**Proposed safe test or comparison:**

**What result would change your mind?**

Please keep critiques specific and evidence-based. Do not submit unreviewed wet-lab procedures or actionable designs for harmful biological agents. Physical experiments belong in qualified institutions under relevant safety review.

## Project status
No formal team, experimental results, validated prototype, or institutional affiliation is implied by this repository. Roles, governance, licensing, and research ethics should be agreed before substantial collaborative work.
