# COLLECTIVE — Provisional Roadmap

**Stage 0 — Concept clarification:** maintain an explicit distinction among known components, hypotheses, and speculative capabilities. Define terms such as intelligence, emergence, novelty, and contribution.

**Stage 1 — Small interdisciplinary review:** invite specialists in synthetic biology, bioelectronics, computer architecture, and collective learning to identify decisive feasibility constraints.

**Stage 2 — Digital proof-of-concept:** model heterogeneous agents with local memory, communication, limited resources, and competing reward policies. Use controlled comparisons and held-out tasks.

**Stage 3 — Interface feasibility:** separately evaluate DNA-addressing/readout, electronic biosensing, metabolism/material handling, and molecular manufacturing. Publish quantitative constraints and negative results.

**Stage 4 — Institutionally supervised modular validation:** if warranted, assess isolated components in appropriately reviewed and contained settings.

**Stage 5 — Integrated research decision:** only proceed if independent component evidence supports integration. Do not presume the system will generate novel organs, replicate, or become intelligent.

### Near-term deliverable
A literature-backed feasibility matrix: **subsystem | demonstrated capability | target capability | gap | evidence | potential test | safety constraints**.
